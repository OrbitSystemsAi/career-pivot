# Business

Non-code business materials for OSai (pitch decks, fundraising docs, go-to-market
materials, etc.), kept separate from `app/`, `core/`, `modules/`, and `docs/` so
engineering and business workstreams don't collide.

- `pitch-deck/` — current pitch deck source assets, notes, and exports (deck itself lives in Google Slides/Canva)

Add new business subfolders here as they come up (e.g. `fundraising/`, `sales/`, `marketing/`).
